# My product demo: pitch (English)

**Rules:** one scene = one screen while it is spoken. Sentences end with . ? or ! and each one is
spoken separately, which keeps captions and highlights in sync. Avoid abbreviations with dots
("e.g.", "Dr.") inside a sentence; they would split it. Digits are fine in English ("10 minutes"),
but words are safer for prices and dates. Abbreviations the voice says wrongly go in project.json
"voice_spellings" (the voice gets "C-S-V", captions keep "CSV").

### Scene 1: Title card
**On screen:** logo, title card.
**Narration:**
Hello, and welcome. Today we will see how to add a new course on this platform.

### Scene 2: The list page
**On screen:** the course list; highlight the add button, then the new row.
**Narration:**
The course list shows every course in one place. Click the new course button to open the form. After saving, the new course appears at the top of the list.

### Scene 3: Closing
**On screen:** closing card.
**Narration:**
Thank you for watching.
